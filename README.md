# ExoticCompactObjectRingdown

This is a collection of the Mathematica notebooks I made for my master thesis within the Master Degree in Astrophysics and Space Physics of Università Milano Bicocca.

They implement the computation of the quasi-normal modes (QNMs) of a background metric. Specifically, Schwarzschild metric for non-rotating black holes (BHs) and exotic compact objects (ECOs), and Kerr metric for rotating BHs and ECOs.

A direct integration numerical method is adopted. Boundary conditions are set in both the event horizon limit (surface in the case of ECOs) and infinity. When achivable, an solution is approximated as a series expansion and used as boundary condition. Otherwise, a more general form is adopted.
